# How Technoblade behaves by default, read by OasisTechnoblade at every start.
# One `name value` line for each setting, all of them required; blank lines
# and lines starting with `#` are left out. Distances are in blocks.
#
# Staff change a setting on the running server with `/technoblade set <name>
# <value>`, which lasts across restarts and new images until `/technoblade
# unset <name>`: those are kept in settings-changed.txt on the world volume,
# and win over the values here. `/technoblade settings` lists what is in force.

# Hitting it. The attacker loses this much health (2 is a heart), though never
# their last half heart, and is thrown back at these speeds across and up, in
# blocks a tick. 0.7 and 0.4 land them about 5 blocks away on flat ground.
hit-damage 6
throw-speed 0.7
throw-lift 0.4

# Feeding it. A player waits this long between golden apples.
feed-cooldown-minutes 5
# The chance of a gold nugget with the apple, and of Luck or Speed with it, is
# 1 in this many each, and the effect lasts this long.
nugget-one-in 10
effect-one-in 10
effect-minutes 2
# After a feed it guards the player for this long: monsters within this far of
# it that go for them go for it instead.
guard-minutes 5
guard-reach 16

# Rumors of where it was seen come this often on average, each one earlier or
# later by up to the jitter, and name a player only within this far of it.
rumor-minutes 30
rumor-jitter-minutes 10
rumor-near 128

# The stare at a player who killed it lasts this long, or until they are
# further than this from it.
stare-seconds 10
stare-reach 32
