Technoblade's crown

The crown's shape and texture come from "Technoblade Pig Crown" by Hohigamer,
version 1.10, published under the MIT License:
https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/technoblade-pig-crown
https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/7gPecjPn/versions/5lpwu4UZ/Technoblade%20Pig%20Crown.zip
(sha256 c1920dd8288451c846a4c0ff1a54d85c7245ea3fde98ad40d628e9172e3d461b)

That pack draws the crown on a pig's head with OptiFine's custom entity
models. Here its 32 boxes, from assets/minecraft/optifine/cem/pig2.jem, are
re-expressed as a vanilla item model (resourcepack/) and a Bedrock geometry
(resourcepack-bedrock/), and the parts of
assets/minecraft/textures/entity/pig/pig_crown.png they use are packed into
one texture, technoblade_crown.png. The positions are moved so the crown sits
on the pig's head as seen from where a passenger rides.
